# Compensation Benchmarking: Strategic Importance & Usage

This document outlines the business value and operational usage of the Compensation Benchmarking module within the Enterprise ERP.

## 1. The Strategic "Use" of Benchmarking

### Gap Analysis
The system automatically calculates the **Comp-Ratio** for every employee.
*   **Formula**: (Actual Total Compensation / Market Median) * 100
*   **Goal**: Maintain employees between 90% and 110% of the market median to ensure competitive health.

### Flight Risk Detection
By cross-referencing salary data with employee tenure and market trends, the system assigns a **Retention Risk Level**:
*   **Critical**: High-tenure employees with a Comp-Ratio below 80%.
*   **High/Medium**: Employees whose pay has stagnated while market rates for their designation have risen.

### Salary Simulation
Before committing to a salary increase, HR can simulate the change on the **Employee Pay** page. The benchmarking widget updates in real-time, showing how the new salary improves the employee's market alignment and reduces their risk score.

## 2. Business Importance for Payroll

| Importance Factor | Strategic Impact |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Reducing Turnover Costs** | Replacing a key employee costs significantly more than a proactive salary adjustment. Benchmarking identifies risks before they become resignations. |
| **Internal Equity** | Prevents pay disparity between peers in the same role, ensuring morale remains high and reducing the risk of legal/compliance disputes regarding pay. |
| **Budget Optimization** | Allows for "Precision Increases." Instead of flat percentage raises for everyone, funds can be targeted toward high-performers who are significantly under-market. |
| **Data-Driven Decision Making** | Removes subjectivity and "gut feelings" from salary reviews. Managers have hard data to back up their compensation decisions during board meetings or annual reviews. |

## 3. Operational Workflow
1.  **Maintain Market Data**: Regularly update the `Salary Benchmarks` table with fresh industry data for your designations.
2.  **Monitor Dashboard**: Check the **Payroll Dashboard** for "Critical Risk" alerts.
3.  **Perform Reviews**: Use the **Benchmarking Widget** on the individual employee pay pages during annual or mid-year review cycles to make informed adjustments.
